Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Skyform app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights you have. We have tried to write it in plain language. If anything is unclear, write to us (see “Contact”).

1. Who is responsible for your data

Skyform is run by Mattia Zorzanello, an individual based in Luxembourg, who is the “data controller” under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • Email: skyform.hq@gmail.com

2. Skyform is for adults

Skyform is only for people aged 18 or over. We ask for your date of birth during sign-up. If you are under 18, you cannot use the app; the phone remembers that this check failed, but we do not keep the date of birth of anyone who is refused.

3. What we collect and why

Your account

  • Email address, and a sign-in method: a one-time code sent to your email, Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google.
  • From Apple: a permanent Apple user ID and an email address (your real one or, if you choose “Hide My Email”, a private relay address from Apple). On your very first Apple sign-in Apple also shares your name; we use it only to pre-fill your first and last name during sign-up and do not store it separately.
  • From Google: a permanent Google user ID, your email address (confirmed by Google), your name and a link to your Google profile picture. We use the name to pre-fill sign-up. The picture link is kept by our sign-in provider as part of your sign-in record; Skyform does not use or show it.
  • We never receive or store a password.

If you sign in with a method whose confirmed email matches an existing Skyform account, both are joined into one account (for example, signing in with Google with the same email you used before).

Why: to create your account and let you sign in (GDPR: needed to provide the service you asked for, Art. 6(1)(b)).

Your profile and goals

First and last name, date of birth, sex, height, weight, measurement units, your goal and training experience.

Why: to calculate your targets and personalise your plan (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Health and fitness data (special category data)

  • Body photos you take or choose, for body-fat estimates and your “Potential” picture.
  • Body-fat estimates and weigh-ins.
  • Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, workout plans.
  • Food: meals you log, food photos, nutrition plans and recipe preferences.
  • Sleep read from Apple Health, only if you allow it. Skyform only reads sleep data; it never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Messages you write to the Coach and the Chef.

Health data is a “special category” under the GDPR. Why: to provide the tracking, plans and coaching you choose to use. You decide what you add: nothing is collected unless you enter it, take or choose a photo, or connect Apple Health (iOS asks your permission before Skyform can read any sleep data). Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), given when you add each kind of data or allow Apple Health access. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account, or by turning off Skyform’s access in your iPhone’s Health settings.

Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold.

AI features (only with your separate permission)

Some features use artificial intelligence from outside providers: the Coach, the Chef, meal recognition from photos, body-fat estimates from photos and the Potential picture. Before any of your data is sent to an AI provider, the app asks your permission for each kind of data:

Usage, errors and bug reports (our own analytics)

We record how the app is used and when it breaks: which screens and features you use, sign-up steps, app errors and crashes, how fast and how reliably our server functions respond, your app version and device model. If you send a bug report, it also includes the last steps you took in the app.

These records never contain body data, food names, message text or photos. They are stored in our own database (no third-party analytics or advertising tools) and are linked to your account so we can understand how the app is used and fix problems.

Why: to keep the app working, fix bugs, and understand which parts are useful (GDPR: our legitimate interest in running and improving the service, Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object by writing to us.

Emails

We send sign-in codes to your email address. We do not send marketing or newsletter emails.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or renting of your data.
  • No sharing with data brokers.
  • No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about you by automated means. AI suggestions (targets, body-fat estimates, coaching) are guidance you can ignore or edit.

5. How long we keep data

  • While you have an account: we keep your data so the app works, until you delete it or your account.
  • When you delete your account (Account > Delete account): your account, profile, goals, weigh-ins, photos, body scans, workouts, food logs, messages, survey answers, analytics and bug reports are deleted immediately from our live systems.
  • Kept after deletion, without anything that identifies you: a count that an account existed (sign-up date and whether it was used), and technical server logs with your user ID removed, which are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Backups: copies of our database in backups are removed within 30 days.
  • AI providers: may keep requests for a limited time for abuse and security monitoring, under their own terms, and then delete them.
  • Apple and Google keep their own record that you used Sign in with Apple / Google with Skyform. You can remove it in your Apple Account or Google Account settings.
  • Inactive accounts: your account and data stay until you delete them, even if you stop using the app. You can delete everything at any time in the app.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy (access, portability);
  • correct it (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete it: in the app at Account > Delete account, or by writing to us;
  • restrict or object to some uses, including our analytics;
  • withdraw consent at any time (AI permissions in Account > Privacy; Apple Health in your iPhone’s Settings). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

Write to skyform.hq@gmail.com. We answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Luxembourg the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), cnpd.public.lu, or the authority where you live.

7. Security

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Each person can only access their own data; the database enforces this. Photos are stored in private storage. Backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authority as the law requires.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
